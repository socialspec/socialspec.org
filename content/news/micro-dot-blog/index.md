> **Thursday, October 15, 2026** &mdash; [Micro.blog] today announced that it has rolled out built-in support for @followspec.org to all @micro.blog users. A new SocialSpec plugin by Micro.blog founder Manton Reece (@manton.org) adds optional support for @aboutspec.org and @contactspec.org.
> 
> Visit [Micro.blog News] and the [Micro.blog Help Center] to learn more.

Manton Reece (@manton.org), the creator of [Micro.blog], has announced built-in support for @followspec.org, which started rolling out to all users less than 48 hours after we announced the SocialSpec project. 🤯
Micro.blog is also offering @aboutspec.org and @contactspec.org support via a new [SocialSpec plugin for Micro.blog].

Manton's work on SocialSpec for Micro.blog is a major milestone for the SocialSpec project because it's the first platform to implement the SocialSpec APIs since the project was announced.[^1]
Thank you, Manton! :pray: 

Check out the [Micro.blog News] announcement, and visit the [Micro.blog Help Center] to learn more.

> ### About Micro.blog
> 
> Micro.blog was created by Manton Reece (@manton.org) and launched in 2017 with a [Kickstarter campaign] supported by 3,000 backers.
> 
> Micro.blog is a unique platform that combines blog hosting with a social media-style timeline and community.
> 
> It’s a paid hosting service for personal blogs that makes it easy to publish short or long posts. Use it for short thoughts, longer essays, photos, podcasts, what books you’re reading, links to news articles, and more.
> 
> It’s a community whose members share posts via a timeline that is a safe space for conversations, free from advertising, trolling, and abuse, with community guidelines that are enforced.

Learn more at https://micro.blog/about &rightarrow;

[^1]: The SocialSpec project was inspired by my work on [HyperTexting]. 
      There are no "first-party" or "third-party" apps for SocialSpec, but HyperTexting did have a head start in that it was the sandbox where I experimented with the ideas that ultimately led to the creation of the SocialSpec project.
